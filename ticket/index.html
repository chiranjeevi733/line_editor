Stadium & Ticketing Guidelines

Ticket Verification

All digital QR tickets and physical passes must be scanned at the designated entry gates at least 90 minutes prior to the match toss. Duplicate or screenshot barcodes will not be accepted.

ID Proof Requirement

Carry a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License) matching the attendee name entered during booking for security verification.

Prohibited Items

Outside food, beverages, power banks, professional cameras, coins, helmets, and sharp objects are strictly prohibited inside the stadium premises.
